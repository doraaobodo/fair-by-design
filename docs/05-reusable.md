# Reusable + Reproducible (20 minutes)

Imagine another team has received permission to access your dataset. What is still needed to reproduce your analysis?

Add a README, provenance notes, processing steps, code/environment versions, quality-control notes, and explicit data-use conditions.

**Open discussion:** Which missing item would most likely lead to a wrong interpretation?
