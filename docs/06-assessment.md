# FAIR Self-Assessment Checklist

**Put FAIR into practice!**

The FAIR Self-Assessment Checklist is an interactive, take-home tool designed to help you evaluate how well your research outputs align with the **4 FAIR principles**—Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable.

Choose a research object, such as a dataset, metadata file, research workflow folder, software repository, or protocol, and work through the checklist to identify what is already FAIR and where improvements are needed.
